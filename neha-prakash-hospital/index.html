A hospital for families

Medical care that feels personal, from the first hello.

Neha Prakash Hospital sets out to give families compassionate, high-quality medical care, and to give it personally, in a place that feels warm and welcoming rather than rushed.

This page is built around what a patient actually needs: which department to go to, how a visit works, where to find the hospital, and a quick way to ask for an appointment.

02Your visit

Four signs from booking to follow-up.

No guesswork on the day. Here is how a visit usually goes, step by step.

  1. 1Book

    Ask for a time

    Send the short form on this page or call 063662 22255, so the front desk can confirm a slot with you.

  2. 2Arrive

    Check in at reception

    Bring earlier prescriptions and reports, and an ID. Reception takes you from there.

  3. 3Consult

    Meet the doctor

    Time to talk it through, ask questions, and agree on what happens next.

  4. 4Reports

    Reports and follow-up

    Collect any test reports, then book your follow-up the same way you booked the first visit.

Reel IV  /  How we work

From hello to highlight film.

Four simple scenes. You plan a wedding; we plan how to remember it.

Sample photo
  1. 01
    Scene one

    Enquiry

    Tell us your names, date, city and events. We'll check the calendar and tell you whether the date is free.

  2. 02
    Scene two

    Planning

    A call to walk through every event, the families, the rituals that matter to you and the shots you can't miss. Then a shot plan for each day.

  3. 03
    Scene three

    The day

    We photograph and film quietly, close to the moments and out of the way, so you can be present with your people.

  4. 04
    Scene four

    Delivery

    Your edited photographs and films, ready to relive and share with family near and far.

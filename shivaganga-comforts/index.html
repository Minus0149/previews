I A letter from the house

Dear traveller, welcome to Balepete.

Step off the train at KSR City and your room is 0.9 km away.

Namaskara,

If you have come to Bengaluru to buy — silk for a wedding, cloth by the bolt, a season’s stock for your own shop — you have come to the right part of town. Chickpet and Balepete are where old Bengaluru still trades, lane after narrow lane of saree houses, textile counters and wholesale shops.

Shivaganga Comforts sits at No. 71, Hospital Road, right next to Swagath Hotel, in the middle of it all. Majestic metro is about 0.4 km away in a straight line, the Majestic bus stand 0.7 km and KSR Bengaluru City railway station 0.9 km. Arrive by train, bus or metro and you can be in your room without crossing the city.

We keep it simple: a room to rest in after a long day in the markets, Wi-Fi to stay in touch with home, and an address that saves you the traffic. Guests on Google have rated their stay 4.8 out of 5 across 84 reviews, the kindest introduction we could ask for.

Write to us or ring the desk on 080 2225 4347, and we will tell you what is free on your dates.

With warm regards,
Shivaganga Comforts
Hospital Road, Balepete

III Around the corner

Chickpet, at your doorstep.

Chickpet is the city’s old trading heart. It is best known for silk sarees, the shop-lined lanes where families come to buy for weddings and festivals, and for the textile and wholesale markets that fill the streets around Balepete.

Staying here means the markets are a walk, not a cab ride. When you are done, the rest of Bengaluru is close too: the metro at Majestic connects you across the city, and the Vidhana Soudha and Cubbon Park are under two kilometres away.

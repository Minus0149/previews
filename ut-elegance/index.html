Welcome

Your base in Gandhi Nagar

UT Elegance sits on Y. Ramchandra Road in Gandhi Nagar, the heart of Bengaluru's travel hub. Step out and you're minutes from the city railway station, the Majestic bus stand and the metro, with Cubbon Park and Vidhana Soudha a short ride away. It's the easy base for a business trip, an early train or a few days in the city.

Good to know

Questions

How do I book a room?

Call the front desk on 080 4854 4447 or send your dates with the form above. Booking directly means no booking-app fees.

How far is the railway station?

KSR Bengaluru City railway station is about 1.0 km away, and the Majestic bus stand about 0.7 km.

How do I get here from the airport?

Kempegowda International Airport is north of the city. The airport buses run to Majestic, a short walk or ride from us. Ask the front desk and we'll guide you.

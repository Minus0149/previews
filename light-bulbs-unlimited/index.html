Bulb guide

Three clues find almost any bulb.

You don't need the model number. Check these three things on the old bulb (or the fixture) and you're most of the way there.

  1. CLUE 01

    The base

    Look where the bulb meets the socket. The everyday screw-in base in U.S. homes is E26 ("medium"). Chandeliers often take the smaller E12 ("candelabra"). Track and recessed lights may use pins instead.

    E26E12E17GU10GU24Bi-pin
  2. CLUE 02

    The brightness

    Brightness is measured in lumens, not watts. As a rough guide, here's what LED bulbs give at common "watt-equivalent" labels:

    40W eq.~450 lm
    60W eq.~800 lm
    75W eq.~1100 lm
    100W eq.~1600 lm
  3. CLUE 03

    The color

    Color temperature is measured in kelvins (K). Lower numbers look warm and golden; higher numbers look crisp and blue-white.

    2700K warm5000K daylight
    Try the color picker

Or skip the homework: bring the old bulb in.Or send a photo of the bulb and its base when the store replies to your request. One more tip: if the light is on a dimmer, ask for a dimmable LED.
